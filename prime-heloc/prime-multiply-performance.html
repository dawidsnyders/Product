How PRIME Multiply Works

PRIME earns ~8% base APY from real US HELOC borrower interest payments. On Kamino, Multiply lets you borrow stablecoins against your PRIME at ~5-7% cost and buy more PRIME — each loop amplifying the yield spread.

Because PRIME yield consistently exceeds stablecoin borrow rates, every layer of leverage adds net profit. At 3x leverage, the net yield rises to 12.45%. At 5x, it reaches 16.64%. At 8x, it compounds to 23.14% — nearly 3x the base yield.

These are real backtested returns over the 76 days since PRIME launched on Kamino (Dec 4, 2025). The positive carry has held across all observed market conditions, with the borrow rate curve engineered to keep the loop profitable up to 97% utilization.

Data period: Dec 4, 2025 — Feb 18, 2026 (76 days). All values are average net APYs after borrow costs. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Leveraged positions carry additional risk including liquidation.
