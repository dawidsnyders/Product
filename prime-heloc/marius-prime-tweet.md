### Jan 13:

First - at surface level, both loopers and lenders want exposure to PRIME's 8% yield. Loopers lever up and earn 8% plus multiplied spread, but take first loss if anything goes wrong.

Lenders earn slightly less but are more senior, protected by loopers getting liquidated first.

The difference between the two of them is akin to junior and senior tranches. Depending on their perception of the PRIME quality, they choose junior or senior exposure (great illustration of the risk/reward tradeoff).

Second - and critical, the yield comes from the same source - HELOC origination activity on Democratized Prime, the lending platform built by Figure.

So, let's recap a few key facts:

1/ PRIME is essentially a tokenized deposit into the Democratized Prime platform - holders of PRIME are equivalent to lenders in Democratized Prime.

2/ PRIME lends to the originators of HELOCs (Home Equity Lines of Credit), NOT directly to homeowners. Originators post the loans as collateral and borrow at 90% LTV against them. The loans stay in DP for an average of ~42 days (or 1.4 homeowner payments). By the time the second payment (around day 60) comes due, the HELOC has typically already been sold into a securitization to institutional investors. Senior tranches of these securitizations keep getting AAA credit ratings.

3/ HELOCs are issued to mid 700s FICO borrowers. This is a solid prime borrower profile, with strong credit histories. They sit below super prime and still carry default risk, but well above the subprime borrowers that drove the 2008 losses.

4/ The value of PRIME declines if losses are incurred, but there are many layers to protect lenders from losses, from overcollateralization to legal recourse and a short exposure window. The realized cumulative gross loss on the entirety of Figure's HELOC originations has been <1.25%.

5/ Figure is the largest non-bank HELOC originator in the US, publicly listed on NASDAQ with $19B+ originated. Democratized Prime was built by Figure to democratize access for lending against these types of collateral assets.

PRIME is an amazing token. It finally brings scalable non-crypto yield to Solana - that's a gigantic step change to what is available on chain.

Digging into it can get really technical, but the good news is that all the data is available, either on chain or on the SEC filings. This is far from some of the other black box yield bearing tokens trading on chain atm.

Lastly, PRIME is obviously not risk free and therefore Kamino users have to choose the kind of exposure they want to it - on the lower-risk side as lenders or holders, or on the higher-risk side as loopers.
