# Kamino Private Credit

Launching today, Kamino Private Credit is a vault that connects  DeFi users on Solana with institutional Bitcoin-backed lending. Private Credit deploys capital to regulated lending operations that make overcollateralized loans to verified institutional borrowers. 

The vault provides a high level of transparency, with real-time collateral and loan-health reporting, and monthly third-party collateral attestation.

The vault operates with conservative risk parameters including **50% maximum LTV ratios** and **no rehypothecation of collateral**. Based on current institutional BTC-backed lending rates, the structure is designed to generate yields in the **6-8% APY range** (actual returns will vary based on lending market conditions).

**The product works as follows:**

1. User deposits USDC into the Private Credit Vault  
2. Funds move through a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) to regulated lending operations. Overcollateralized BTC-backed Loans are originated to institutional borrowers.   
3. Collateral remains secure at qualified custodians under a tripartite agreement, with no title transfer to the borrower. Reporting transparency will be maintained throughout, and 3rd party attestation will be available to Kamino users  
4. Returns from the Lending activity flow back to vault depositors.

**Kamino Private Credit differs from typical DeFi lending in several important ways:**

| Attribute | Traditional DeFi | Kamino Private Credit |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Yield Source | Onchain borrow demand | Institutional BTC-backed lending |
| Underlying Borrower Profile | Pseudonymous | KYC-verified institutional entities |
| Collateral Location | Smart contracts | Qualified Custodians |
| Collateral Coverage | Up to 86% | 50% LTV minimum (2:1 coverage) |
| Regulatory Oversight | None | Switzerland FINMA / Liechtenstein FMA |
| Liquidity | Instant, utilization-dependent (utilization typically \~85%)  | Hybrid (5-10% instant \+ queued) |
| Rates & Term | Floating based on supply/demand | Fixed Rates, Fixed Term |

## Getting Started

### Deposits

Deposit USDC to receive vault share tokens at current vault Net Asset Value (NAV). Share value appreciates as the vault earns interest from lending activities. The process is permissionless \- deposit, receive shares, accrue value. As part of the Deposit process, you will be asked to agree to  a Loan Agreement with the Private Credit SPV and accept the terms. 

### Withdrawals

Instant redemption is available up to the 5-10% liquidity buffer (explained in the operational information section below). Liquidity will be managed such that a liquidity buffer is maintained. If withdrawal demand exceeds the liquidity buffer, there may be a waiting period until underlying loans come to term and are repaid. Loans are originated on a series of rolling 60 Day  duration.

# Operational Information

### 1\. Product Terms

Kamino Private Credit’s Lending Parameters are calibrated for user safety, and to ensure that funds are deployed to Lending Operations that meet various criteria for high quality of operation:

| Parameter | Terms |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Typical Yield** | 6–8% APY |
| **Deposit Currency** | USDC |
| **Instant Liquidity Buffer** | 5-10% of AUM |
| **Redemption Model** | Hybrid (instant buffer \+ queued) |
| **Underlying Collateral** | BTC only  |
| **Maximum LTV** | 50% |
| **Collateral Rehypothecation** | Prohibited. BTC title remains with the Borrower |
| **Regulatory Oversight** | Counterparties regulated under FMA (Liechtenstein) FMA with Swiss operations.  |

### 2\. Reporting and Transparency

Institutional BTC-Backed yields introduce a reliance on external lenders and custodians. Thus, the vault ensures that: funds are deployed only to highly regulated institutions in top tier jurisdictions; collateral is always in qualified custodians; and high levels of transparency are maintained at all times enabled by continuous reporting.

Reporting will include the following:

* **Collateral Position Reporting** — Ongoing reporting attesting the aggregate BTC collateral value backing loans  
* **Real-Time Loan Status Reporting** — Ongoing reporting that attest to active loan amounts, weighted average LTVs, and overall loan health, powered by [Accountable](https://www.accountable.capital/)  
* **Third-Party Attestations** — Independent verification of positions by professional accounting firms, on a monthly recurring basis

### 3\. Liquidity Management

Kamino’s Private Credit vault structure maintains multiple liquidity management layers to manage redemptions:

- **Kamino Vault maintains 5-10% instant liquidity** for immediate redemptions  
- **Defined loan maturities** enable predictable liquidity planning; Kamino interface reflects time to liquidity   
- **FIFO redemption queue** processes excess redemptions in orderly fashion when buffer is exhausted (feature to be introduced Q2/Q3 2026\)
