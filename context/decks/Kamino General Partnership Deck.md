## Design System Reference

**Colors:**
- Primary backgrounds: White (#FFFFFF)
- Secondary backgrounds: Ice blue (#D4F1F9)
- Accent (sparingly): Orange (#FF6B35) — used for one highlight card per slide max
- Dark backgrounds: Black (#000000) — statement slides, disclaimers, partner band
- Text: Black on light, White on dark
- Subtle/secondary text: Light gray (#999999)

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Geometric grotesque sans-serif, bold, very large scale (titles occupy 1/3 to 1/2 of slide)
- Body: Same family, regular weight, significantly smaller
- Labels: UPPERCASE, small, tracked out (e.g. "TRUSTED BY", "LAUNCHING WITH")
- Stats: Large bold numerals in stat cards

**Layout Patterns Used:**
- Split (40/60 or 50/50): Most slides
- Stat cards: Light blue rectangles, large number + label
- Comparison tables: Clean rows, Kamino column highlighted in light blue
- Numbered steps: "01, 02, 03, 04" bold with descriptions
- Three-band: White / light blue / black horizontal sections
- Full-black statement: Centered text on black
- Flow diagrams: Gray rounded containers with partner logos
- Venn diagrams: Overlapping circles for conceptual relationships

**Recurring Elements:**
- Kamino logo: Bottom-left of every content slide
- Page numbers: Bottom-right, small gray
- Abstract product icons: Unique dotted/lined motifs per section
- Partner logos: In their native/original format
- Photography: Cinematic, institutional, motion-blurred (buildings, money, people)
