### Figure Technology Solutions
- **Handle:** @figure
- **Website:** https://www.figure.com/
- **One-line identity:** Publicly-listed (Nasdaq: FIGR) blockchain-native consumer credit originator and the largest non-bank home equity lender in the United States.
- **What they do:** Originate HELOCs, cash-out refinance, DSCR loans and crypto-backed loans on their own Provenance Blockchain, and tokenize those loans so institutional buyers can trade and finance them on-chain. - **Recent / 2026:** IPO'd on Nasdaq in September 2025 at a ~$7.6B valuation after merging with Figure Markets. Reporting Q1 2026 results on May 11, 2026. Expanding the RWA Consortium around PRIME.
