## Custody

### Anchorage Digital
- **Relationship type:** Strategic integration — qualified-custodian partner for Kamino's Off-Chain Collateral product
- **Date of public announcement:** February 13, 2026 (joint announcement with Solana Company / HSDT).
- **Kamino products involved:** Off-Chain Collateral (brand-new product); Kamino Lend reserves for stablecoin borrowing; Anchorage's Atlas collateral management system orchestrates LTV, margin movements, and rules-based liquidations 24/7.
- **Status / scale:** Pilot live Feb 2026. Solana Company (NASDAQ: HSDT) became the first digital asset treasury to borrow against ~$201M of natively-staked SOL while collateral stays in Anchorage Digital Bank N.A. under a tri-party account control agreement.

By partnering with Anchorage Digital, Kamino enables institutions to access on-chain liquidity and yield on Solana while continuing to custody assets within their existing regulated framework." — Cheryl Chan, Head of Strategy, Kamino
