## What you would own

The work is infrastructure other companies build their products on: a lending engine, the [Scope](./scope.md) pricing oracle, an access-control layer, a liquidation engine, a best-execution router, a vault engine, and a cross-chain bridge. One shared stack composes into a consumer app anyone can use and the credit products funds and issuers underwrite. Engineers here own whole systems end to end and see how the machine fits together. [How Kamino works](./how-kamino-works.md) walks through the full picture.

---

## The kind of work this is

**Correctness with consequences.** Every system moves capital, so a missed edge case is a solvency event. The track record is the standard we hold: $0 bad debt across more than 100,000 liquidations, zero oracle exploits across the value Scope has priced, three years in production. We build to keep that record clean — rolling audits, formal verification, reproducible on-chain builds, continuous fuzzing. The full diligence picture lives on the [Security](./security.md) page.

**Whole-machine breadth.** A single product — a fixed-rate institutional credit facility, a leveraged position, a BTC-backed yield vault — composes the lending engine, the oracle, permissioning, liquidations, the router, and the vault engine at once. Owning a system means understanding how it behaves alongside all the others under real load.

**Customers who push the rails.** PRIME took a tokenized private-credit asset past $1B in deposits on Kamino's markets. FalconX, Pantera, and Anchorage deploy against the institutional layer. Phantom and OneKey embed Kamino in their own apps. The work ships to counterparties who exercise every parameter we expose. The [Customers](./customers.md) page has the stories.

## How we work

- **Small team, high ownership.** Surface area per person is large. You carry whole systems from design through audit to production, accountable for how they behave under load.
- **Solana-native, Ethereum-aware.** The core runs on Solana; the [cross-chain bridge](./cross-chain.md) reaches Ethereum-native assets so liquidations and rebalances happen where the liquidity lives. Depth in either ecosystem is welcome.
- **Built in the open.** The contracts are open source with reproducible builds, the audit reports are public, and governance runs through multisig you can watch live. The work stands up to outside scrutiny because it is built to be looked at.

## Who tends to do well here

People who thrive here care about correctness the way a good trader cares about a fill — precisely, and with money on the line. They own a system end to end, reason about adversarial edge cases, and write code an auditor will read line by line. They come from protocol and smart-contract engineering, risk and quantitative research, oracle and infrastructure work, product design, and operating roles where they have run real capital. What they share is a high bar for their own work and an appetite for the responsibility that comes with infrastructure other people trust their balance sheets to.

## Roles we hire for

- **Protocol engineering** — Rust on Solana: the lending engine, vault engine, liquidation engine, and on-chain routing and limit-order systems.
- **Oracle and infrastructure engineering** — Scope's pricing layer, feed integrations, the guard stack, and redundancy across the RPC, oracle, and liquidator layers.
- **Risk and quantitative research** — market parameterization, VaR analysis, volatility and price-shock modeling, the risk dashboard, and monthly risk reporting.
- **Front-end and product engineering** — the consumer app, the curator and institutional surfaces, and the SDKs and APIs integrators build against.
- **Product design** — making credit, leverage, and yield legible for both a first-time consumer and a professional curator.
- **Institutional and partnerships** — onboarding issuers, funds, treasuries, and fintechs onto the rails, and the white-glove motion behind every bespoke market.
