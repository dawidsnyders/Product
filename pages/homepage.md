## What Kamino is

Kamino runs the three functions every capital market needs — lending, trading, and asset management — as a single configurable system on Solana. A market is a venue you tune; a vault is the supply behind it; an oracle prices what flows through both. Tell us the asset, the counterparties, the access rules, and the terms, and the infrastructure produces the venue. A bespoke institutional market is a configuration of settings already in production.

It runs at scale today: $2.5B in assets under management, $21B+ in loans originated, $120M+ in interest generated, and $0 bad debt across more than three years. It is the largest real-world-asset credit platform in on-chain finance, with a $1B RWA market across six live markets spanning home-equity loans, tokenized equities, money markets, and reinsurance-backed assets. When an institution lists an asset here, it joins markets already operating at that scale.

That is what lets one platform serve the whole chain of capital:

- An **institution** borrows against Bitcoin held in custody, at a rate locked to maturity.
- A **wealth manager** allocates client capital into regulated, transparent yield and runs a managed mandate on the same rails.
- An **asset issuer** lists a tokenized fund or equity, gets it priced, made borrowable, and tradable from day one.
- A **fintech** embeds yield and credit into its own app through an API, with compliance-grade access control built in.
- An **individual** earns, borrows, multiplies, and swaps directly, with the same security underneath.

## What you can build on

Three capability areas sit on top of one infrastructure stack. Each is a hub; below each are the products a buyer selects.

### Credit — borrow against anything, at fixed or floating rates

Every loan happens in a market you tune across roughly fifty reserve parameters: caps, loan-to-value, the interest-rate curve, oracle source, term, and access rules. Four of those choices set the shape — how assets are risk-grouped, whether the venue is open or gated, fixed-term or floating, on-chain or custodied — and span the full range from a retail borrow market to a KYC-gated institutional credit line.

- **[Fixed Rates →](./fixed-rates.md)** — Lock a rate to maturity across 1-, 3-, 6-, and 12-month terms. A 5.5% three-month loan charges 5.5% to maturity whatever the variable market does, and at term it rolls forward automatically. The spread across durations forms a live, on-chain yield curve.
- **[Custodial Borrowing →](./custodial-borrowing.md)** — Borrow on-chain against assets held at a qualified custodian. Collateral ownership stays with the borrower, the custodian holds it under an account-control agreement, and rehypothecation is contractually prohibited.
- **[Permissioned Markets →](./permissioned-markets.md)** — KYC-gated, allowlisted credit venues. Approval is required to enter; withdraw and repay always stay open.
- **[Multiply →](./multiply.md)** — One-click leverage looping up to 10x, with fixed-rate Multiply to lock the cost of the loop for its term.

Powered by [Markets](./markets.md), priced by [Scope](./scope.md), kept safe by the [Liquidation engine](./liquidation-engine.md).
**[Explore Credit →](./credit.md)**

### Liquidity — best-execution trading and on-chain liquidity

A meta-aggregator ranks routes across the major Solana venues and a proprietary on-chain router, returning best execution to every swap, leverage loop, and repayment in the platform.

- **[Kamino Swap →](./kamino-swap.md)** — Best-execution swaps that power Multiply, debt management, and repayment underneath.
- **[RFQ →](./rfq.md)** — Solver-filled quotes that move size in and out of less-liquid tokenized assets, with market makers integrating as solvers.
- **[RWA Liquidity →](./rwa-liquidity.md)** — Oracle-priced trading for tokenized assets, where the oracle price is the liquidity price, so the pool quotes both sides itself and every trade clears against the oracle directly.
- **[Liquidity Vaults →](./liquidity-vaults.md)** — Automated concentrated-liquidity management with range-setting, rebalancing, and auto-compounding.

### Yield — curated and managed on-chain yield

Every vault is run by a named curator who sets the strategy, posts first-loss capital, and answers for the risk. Deposits compound automatically; the capital behind them supplies the credit markets borrowers draw on.

- **[Lending Vaults →](./lending-vaults.md)** — Single-asset vaults run by curators like Gauntlet, Steakhouse, and Allez, allocating across markets within set weights and caps.
- **[Meta-Vaults →](./meta-vaults.md)** — A configurable multi-asset, multi-strategy vehicle: a managed fund in a box, for wealth managers and fintechs.
- **[Institutional Yield →](./institutional-yield.md)** — A stablecoin yield from overcollateralized Bitcoin-backed lending through a regulated lender, with a real-time loan-book feed, weekly reporting, and monthly independent attestation.
- **[Permissioned Vaults →](./permissioned-vaults.md)** — Deposits flow in from a fixed allowlist of approved wallets or a single named source, for regulated mandates and compliant fintech yield.

### Build on Kamino

The platform is two-sided. Operate a venue on Kamino's rails, or embed Kamino into your product.

- **[Curation →](./curation.md)** — Run your own market or vault: set the parameters, fees, allocations, and access rules; appear on Kamino's consumer surface, or run white-label on your own. Curators keep their performance and AUM fees; Kamino's revenue comes from the protocol take rate on the underlying lending.
- **[Yield API →](./institutional-yield-api.md)** — Productized yield a partner integrates into its own stack as a tailored Meta-Vault structure, with a configurable user cap, revenue share, and a full embed. The partner's clients hold balances in the partner's own branded stablecoin, issued via Coinbase's stablecoin-as-a-service, and that stablecoin is the unit of account those balances earn on.
- **[Launch your own on-chain money market →](./uc-own-stablecoin-market.md)** — Stand up a closed loop on Kamino: tokenize a fund, deploy it into a white-glove permissioned market that carries your own identity and authorization rules, and use your own branded stablecoin — issued via Coinbase's stablecoin-as-a-service — as the borrow liquidity. Holders lever the fund against the stablecoin; the clients who supply that stablecoin earn the yield the loopers pay. The loop closes on the stablecoin yield, and your clients sit on both sides of it.
- **[BuildKit →](./buildkit.md)** — Embed Kamino credit, yield, and swap into your app in a few lines, with compliance-grade permissioning built in.

## Why the breadth holds together

Credit and yield are one machine. Vaults pool depositor capital and supply it into markets; borrowers draw on it and pay interest back to those vaults. Conditional allocation lets a vault earn floating yield while standing ready to fund a fixed-rate term loan. The same capital earns variable yield in the market right up to the instant a borrower arrives, then moves to fund the term loan in a single step, so a large institutional borrow is filled the moment it lands and the supply behind it works for lenders every minute in between.

Underneath sits the hardest part to replicate: **[Scope](./scope.md)**, the in-house pricing engine that took years to build and prices the hard assets a general-purpose oracle leaves on the table — tokenized credit, equities, NAV-based funds, gold. It composes around forty-two price types. It takes the freshest of several sources within a hard divergence limit, caps and floors, discounts term instruments to maturity, honors equity market hours and NAV pauses, and freezes any asset whose supply spikes against its own average. Whatever Scope can price safely, Kamino can lend, borrow, loop, and trade. Pricing is the gate to the entire tokenized-asset universe, and the [Cross-chain bridge](./cross-chain.md) extends it off Solana, liquidating Ethereum-native collateral on deep Ethereum venues so tokenized gold and dollar-yield assets are safe as Solana collateral.
