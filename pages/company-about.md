## Who we are

Kamino is a capital-markets infrastructure company. The platform launched on Solana in November 2023 and has run continuously since, growing into the venue where tokenized assets become financeable: $2.5B in assets under management, over $21B in loans originated, a $1B real-world-asset market spanning six live markets, and three years live with zero bad debt and zero security incidents.

The company exists because tokenization is happening at the asset layer first, and the layer beneath it was missing. A fund tokenizes its private-credit book. A custodian issues a wrapped Bitcoin. An issuer mints a tokenized treasury or a tokenized equity. Each is a real claim sitting on-chain as a token, and each issuer and holder runs into the same question next: what can I actually *do* with it? Kamino is the answer to that question — the priced, financeable, governed infrastructure that the rest of the market builds on top of.

## The thesis

A token becomes useful capital once there is infrastructure underneath it: a way to price it safely, borrow against it, trade in and out of it, lend it into yield, and do all of that under the access controls a regulated balance sheet requires. That infrastructure is what Kamino builds, and it starts from a single conviction — whatever can be priced safely can be financed safely. Get the pricing right, and credit, liquidity, and yield follow.

The pricing engine, [Scope](./scope.md), values assets with real-world behavior that generic crypto oracles leave on the table: tokenized equities with market hours and trading halts, NAV-based funds with redemption pauses, gold, private-credit instruments, and Bitcoin held at a custodian. It wraps each price in a guard stack that turns oracle risk into bounded, governed risk. Whatever Scope can price, the rest of the platform can lend against, loop, and trade. That is the gate to the entire tokenized-asset universe, and the moat under everything else.

## How we work with institutions

Kamino stays on the rails alongside the institution well past integration. The oracle that prices an asset, the risk parameters on its market, and the integration into custody and KYB systems are run by the team that built and operates them: pricing logic stays under Kamino's control, markets and vaults are set up white-glove and tuned to the asset, the access rules, and the mandate, and collateral held at a qualified custodian is read through a tri-party agreement with on-chain attestations. The result is one accountable counterparty across pricing, risk, custody integration, and support, with the SLAs and reporting to hold it to those outcomes. End to end, every dollar of yield traces back to a KYB-verified borrower.

## What we've built

Kamino is one integrated platform. [Credit](./credit.md) lets a holder borrow against any priced asset at fixed or floating rates, inside [configurable markets](./markets.md) tuned across roughly fifty risk parameters. [Liquidity](./liquidity.md) returns best execution through a meta-aggregating router and gives tokenized assets a tradable price from the moment they list. [Yield](./yield.md) puts depositor capital to work through vaults run by named professional curators, including [regulated Bitcoin-backed credit yield](./institutional-yield.md) with a real-time loan-book feed and monthly independent attestation.

These are one machine. The [vault engine](./vault-engine.md) pools depositor capital and routes it into the markets that lend it; the same supply commits to a fixed-rate term loan at the moment it funds, so depositor capital keeps earning right up until the loan draws. Credit and yield are two faces of one flow of money.

Everything on the platform reaches the market three ways. A buyer uses it directly through the [Kamino app](./individuals.md) or the institutional product surface; has it built for them, where Kamino's team stands up a market or vault tuned to their asset, access rules, and mandate through configuration alone; or [builds it in](./buildkit.md), embedding Kamino's credit, yield, and swap inside their own product or [operating a venue](./curation.md) on the rails under their own brand.
