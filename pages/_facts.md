## Own branded stablecoin (cross-cutting offering — founder-confirmed)
A partner can run its product on Kamino under **its own branded stablecoin** (issued via **Coinbase's stablecoin-as-a-service**), in place of a third-party stablecoin. This packages across the suite:
- **Yield API** — the partner's stablecoin is the unit of account; balances flow into a tailored Meta-Vault structure and earn.
- **The closed loop (flagship institutional story):** a tokenized fund → deployed into a **white-glove, permissioned market** carrying the institution's own identity and authorization rules → the institution's **own branded stablecoin** as the borrow liquidity → **yield on that stablecoin paid back to the institution's clients.** Two sides of one loop: holders lever the fund against the stablecoin; the stablecoin liquidity providers (also the institution's clients) earn the yield the loopers pay. The loop is closed by the stablecoin yield.
