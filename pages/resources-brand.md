## Logos and marks

The kit ships every asset in both **SVG** and **PNG**, in light and dark variants so a mark always sits cleanly on its background.

| Asset | Variants | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wordmark** | Black · White | The full "Kamino" lockup — headers, footers, partner pages, press. |
| **Icon** | Black · White | The standalone symbol — avatars, app tiles, favicons, tight spaces. |
| **KMNO token** | Black · White | The governance-token mark — exchange listings, token lists, governance contexts. |

Pick the variant that reads cleanly against your background: the **White** wordmark and icon for dark surfaces, **Black** for light surfaces. Give every mark room to breathe — keep clear space around it equal to the height of the icon, and place the mark on a solid or low-contrast field so the lettering and symbol stay legible.

## Color

Three core colors carry the brand. **Paper** and **Black** do the working background and text; **Kamino Blue** is the accent that signals the brand. Pull the exact values from the swatches in the [brand kit](https://kamino.com/brand) so every partner ships the same color.

| Color | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Paper** | Primary light background and large surfaces. |
| **Kamino Blue** | Highlights and brand accents. |
| **Black** | Dark backgrounds and primary text. |

Reserve Kamino Blue for accents and moments of emphasis so it stays distinctive. For body text and large surfaces, lean on Paper and Black.

**Legible pairings.** Set dark text on Paper and on Kamino Blue; set Paper or white text on Black. Kamino Blue is a light tint, so it carries dark text and dark marks well and should never sit under white text. The kit includes a working neutral ramp — the grays for borders, dividers, secondary text, and surfaces — so partner UI draws from one shared set of grays. Pull those from the kit alongside the core colors.

## Typography

Kamino sets its interface and brand type in **Aeonik**, the CoType Foundry grotesque that gives the brand its voice across web, app, and print. The weight ramp runs **Light** for large display lines, **Regular** for body, **Medium** for UI labels and emphasis, and **Semibold/Bold** for headings and strong callouts; **Medium** is the everyday workhorse for product UI. Where a system fallback is needed before the webfont loads, fall back through `Inter, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif`. The Aeonik webfonts ship in the [brand kit](https://kamino.com/brand) for licensed use in Kamino-branded materials.

## Using the marks well

- **Use the assets as supplied.** Keep the proportions, the colors, and the spacing intact.
- **Keep clear space** around every mark — at minimum the height of the icon on all sides.
- **Choose contrast.** Light marks on dark fields, dark marks on light fields.
- **Spell it Kamino.** One word, capital K, every time.
- For co-branding, partner lockups, or any use the kit doesn't cover, reach out to **press@kamino.com** and we'll help you get it right.
