## Choose the door that fits

Three motions, depending on how you want to operate. Most institutions begin with the white-glove engagement and graduate into running their own venue or embedding Kamino once the first deployment is live.

### White-glove deployment

We stand up the venue with you. A solutions engineer scopes your asset, your risk tolerance, your access rules, and your reporting requirements, then configures the market or vault to match. Custody integration, oracle onboarding, KYC gating, and a go-live review are part of the package. This is how funds borrow at fixed rates, how treasuries borrow against custodied BTC, and how wealth managers allocate into [Institutional Yield →](./institutional-yield.md).

The economics live in the deployment you run: you pay the protocol take rate on the lending that flows through your venue, and your specialist prices it against your expected volume on the scoping call.

### Run your own venue

Launch and operate a market or vault yourself on Kamino's audited rails through the [Curation Suite →](./curation.md). Set parameters, allocations, fees, and access rules; turn on the safety controls; pass a go-live review to surface on Kamino's consumer app and reach its demand. You keep the performance and AUM fees you set for your depositors, and Kamino earns the protocol take rate on the underlying lending. Sales helps you scope the venue and routes you to the right onboarding.

### Embed Kamino in your product

Bring Kamino's credit, yield, and swap into your own app. This is for fintechs and platforms that want to offer their users earning, borrowing, and swapping as native features of their own product while Kamino's audited rails do the protocol work underneath. You stand up the flows through [BuildKit →](./buildkit.md), which integrates the core actions in a few lines, or run yield-in-a-box through the [Yield API →](./institutional-yield-api.md), with a configurable user cap and revenue share, optionally under your own stablecoin. You keep your brand, your front end, and your user relationship, while compliant permissioning screens users through the whitelist API. Engineers can start in the [Developers hub →](./developers.md); sales scopes the commercial terms and the integration plan.

## What to expect

The institutional motion is built to move at the speed of your diligence, with a single point of contact the whole way.

**1 — Scoping call.** A solutions engineer who knows your segment maps what you're trying to do to the products that serve it. You leave with a concrete shape: the market or vault configuration, the access model, the custody arrangement, the reporting you'll receive, and a first read on the economics for your motion.

**2 — Diligence and security review.** Security is the gating question when you're underwriting custody of institutional capital, so we put the full picture in front of you early: 20 external audits, 4 formal verifications, three years in production without an incident, and $0 of bad debt across more than $120M liquidated in over 100,000 liquidations. Two live dashboards stay open to you — every multisig proposal, signature, and timelock at multisig.kamino.fi, and every oracle feed and source at oracles.kamino.fi. The full diligence pack lives on [Security →](./security.md).

**3 — Configuration and onboarding.** Your venue is configured to the agreed spec. Asset onboarding starts with building a guarded [Scope →](./scope.md) price feed for your collateral; the [market →](./markets.md) is tuned across its risk parameters; [Permissioning →](./permissioning.md) gates deposit, borrow, or liquidate per market, operation, wallet, or asset, with KYC verified off-chain. Custodied collateral is wired to a qualified custodian under a tripartite account-control agreement, ownership stays with you, and the custodian is contractually barred from rehypothecating the collateral.

**4 — Go-live.** Launch is staged so your first capital enters under control. The venue can run hidden first — fully live on-chain, invisible in the app — so you and a small set of counterparties can transact in a private preview before public listing. Monitoring is live from the first transaction: the liquidation engine, the danger-trigger safety controls, and the oracle guards are all running the moment the venue opens. Your solutions engineer is on the call through launch and watches the first positions settle with you.

**5 — Ongoing partnership.** Transparency is built into the live product. For [Institutional Yield →](./institutional-yield.md), depositors see the same per-loan data the operation files in its regulatory reporting, a real-time view of the liquidity buffer in the vault UI, weekly reporting, and a monthly attestation signed by an independent accounting firm. Your point of contact stays with you for new assets, new venues, and scaling.

## The trust that backs the conversation

Institutions deploy on Kamino because the safety envelope is verifiable and the track record is public. The headline diligence numbers sit in step 2 above; here is the mechanism behind each of them.

- **The asset universe is gated by what can be priced safely.** [Scope →](./scope.md) composes more than 40 price types — taking the freshest of several sources with hard divergence limits, capping and flooring prices, discounting fixed-term instruments to maturity, and running bespoke RWA valuation. It handles equity market hours and halts, suspends pricing through corporate-action windows, honors NAV pauses, and freezes an asset's usable price if its supply spikes against its own average. Scope has processed $19.33B with zero oracle exploits.
- **The liquidation engine clears stress predictably.** Auction-based liquidations, soft auto-deleverage, and fixed-rate maturity liquidations resolve underwater positions through open competition among whitelisted liquidators, which is how the clean record above was earned through real market drawdowns. Read the engine on [Liquidation engine →](./liquidation-engine.md).
- **Compliance is a setting on live infrastructure.** [Permissioning →](./permissioning.md) is additive and reversible: a single configuration change gates or ungates a venue while it keeps running, layered with whitelisted reserves, a curator-funded insurance pool, and qualified custody, mapping onto how regulated capital actually allocates.
- **Named institutions already run here.** FalconX borrows at fixed rates. A major crypto fund borrows against custodied collateral. PRIME crossed $1B in tokenized RWA deposits. Anchorage provides custody. Marinade runs a white-label vault; Phantom and OneKey embed Kamino. See the full set on [Customers →](./customers.md).

## Frequently asked, before you call

**How fast can we go live?** Because a bespoke market or vault is configuration on audited rails, the venue configuration itself is fast — the price feed, the tuned market, the permissioning rules, and the custody wire are all assembled from components that are already live. The timeline is set by your side of the diligence: KYC provider onboarding, custody agreements, and your internal sign-off. Your specialist maps that path on the first call and tells you which items gate the date.

**What does it cost?** You pay when the product earns, and the economics differ by motion. In a white-glove deployment you run, you pay the protocol take rate on the lending that flows through your venue. When you run your own venue, you keep the performance and AUM fees you set for depositors while Kamino earns the protocol take rate on the underlying lending. When you embed through the Yield API, it carries a configurable revenue share. Your specialist walks through the numbers for your specific motion, and each product page states its economics inline.

**Can we keep custody of our collateral?** Yes. [Custodial Borrowing →](./custodial-borrowing.md) lets you borrow on-chain against assets held at a qualified custodian under a tripartite account-control agreement, with on-chain proof of reserves and a contractual bar on rehypothecation of your collateral.

**Can we gate our venue to approved counterparties only?** Yes. Permissioning gates who can deposit, borrow, and liquidate down to the individual wallet and asset, with KYC verified off-chain so the on-chain layer stays identity-agnostic.
